Privacy Policy

Academic project notice

VitalTrek is a student project developed by Nexum Devs for the course 1ASI0730 Aplicaciones Web at Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas. It is a minimum viable product built for academic evaluation. It is not a commercial service, no real expedition is monitored through it, and this document is an academic exercise in drafting service terms — not legal advice.

Scope of this Policy

This Policy explains how the Nexum Devs team would handle personal data in the VitalTrek platform and on this website. It follows the principles of Peru's Ley N.° 29733, Ley de Protección de Datos Personales, and its regulations.

In the monitoring service, the tour operator decides why and how participant data is processed. VitalTrek acts on the operator's instructions and only for the purposes described here.

Information we process

Operator account data

Name of the organization, contact person, work email address and the role each staff member holds in the platform.

Expedition data

Route and checkpoint definitions, group composition, expected schedules, the first name or identifier of each participant and an emergency contact provided by the operator.

Field telemetry

Readings captured by the wearable device — position at the moment of synchronization, timestamps, movement indicators and device battery and status — transmitted when a group reaches a Bluetooth checkpoint.

Website data

This landing page does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The only thing it stores in the browser is the language you choose, kept locally so the site opens in the same language next time.

Why we process it

Data is processed to show operators the progress of their groups, to flag groups that are behind schedule, to support decisions during an incident, and to produce a record of the expedition afterwards. We also use aggregated, non-identifying information to improve the product as coursework.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Location and health data

Location and physical activity data are sensitive. They are collected only while an expedition is active, only at the level of detail the safety of the group requires, and are never used to evaluate a participant for any purpose unrelated to that expedition.

Under Peruvian law, sensitive personal data requires the free, prior, informed and explicit consent of the person concerned. The operator must obtain that consent before the expedition begins.

Consent of participants

Every participant must be told, in a language they understand, what the device records, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to withdraw consent. Consent is given freely and can be withdrawn at any moment without affecting the participant's right to join the tour.

If a participant withdraws consent, the operator stops the collection and the expedition continues under the operator's ordinary safety procedures.

Sharing information

Expedition data is visible to the authorized staff of the operator that runs the expedition. It may also be shared with emergency or rescue services when there is a serious risk to a person's life or health, and with the authorities when a legal obligation requires it.

Hosting and deployment rely on third-party infrastructure providers, which process the data only to keep the service running.

How long we keep it

Expedition records are retained while they are useful to the operator for safety review, and are then deleted or anonymized. Account data is kept while the organization uses the platform.

Because VitalTrek is an academic MVP, any data stored during the course may be deleted when the academic term ends.

Your rights

Any person whose data is processed may exercise the rights recognized by Ley N.° 29733:

  • Access — to know what data about them is held and how it is used.
  • Rectification — to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Cancellation — to have their data deleted when it is no longer needed.
  • Objection — to refuse a processing they have legitimate grounds to oppose.

Requests should be addressed to the operator that organized the expedition, which can reach the Nexum Devs team through this site.

Security

Access to the platform is restricted by role, traffic is served over HTTPS, and the team follows reviewed pull requests and version control so that every change to the code is traceable.

No system is completely secure. As an academic MVP, VitalTrek should not be used to store data that an organization could not afford to lose or expose.

This website

This landing page collects no personal information. It does not use analytics or advertising cookies, and it sets no tracking identifiers.

It stores a single preference in your browser's local storage — the language you selected. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Minors

When a participant is under 18, the consent described in this Policy must be given by a parent or legal guardian, and the operator is responsible for obtaining and keeping that record.

Contact

Questions about this Policy can be sent to the Nexum Devs team through the contact section of this site.
